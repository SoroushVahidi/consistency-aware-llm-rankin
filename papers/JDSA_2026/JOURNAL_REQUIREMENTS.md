# Official JDSA requirements checked 26 October 2026

Sources read: [complete instructions](https://link.springer.com/journal/41060/submission-guidelines), [publishing route](https://link.springer.com/journal/41060/how-to-publish-with-us), [Springer Nature template page](https://www.springernature.com/gp/authors/campaigns/latex-author-support), and the linked publisher journal policies. Local complete extracted pages are retained under `audit/official_*.txt`, excluded from the versioned source package. `audit/TEMPLATE_PROVENANCE.json` records the official December 2024 template download and its hash. The class/style are copied unchanged from that download.

The instructions specify Regular Paper as original research involving new theories, methods or technologies. This manuscript argues for an empirical evaluation methodology contribution; acceptance as a Regular Paper is an editorial judgment, not established by formatting. Two-column formatting is specified both as `twocolumn` in the general preparation text and `iicol` for the current Springer template; the latter is used. Generic Springer `sn-basic,Numbered` produces the required numbered square-bracket citations and full DOI links. The older `spbasic.bst` suggestion is reconciled with the currently recommended Nature template's corresponding basic style, rather than mixing incompatible classes.

The title page must identify authors, affiliations, an active corresponding email and ORCID if available. Abstract: 150–250 words; keywords: 4–6. Editable sources, style files, figures and compiled PDFs are required; source archives must have no subfolders. Main section numbering is decimal with at most three levels. Figures/tables require ordered in-text citations and descriptive captions. Vector figure fonts must be embedded. The existing numerical artwork is preserved; program identity is Matplotlib/PDF and the inherited layout-only PyMuPDF formatting script; Figure 1 is editable TikZ. No AI-generated bitmap is added.

Supplementary PDFs are published as supplied, use separate numbering, include the article/journal/author/corresponding metadata, and are cited as Online Resource. This package provides `ESM_1.pdf` and a descriptive main-text caption. Single-column SI preserves diagnostic-table legibility; the main paper uses `iicol`.

Data availability is mandatory. Funding/interests, contributions and applicable ethics/consent disclosures must be included and entered consistently into the interface. AI use beyond copy editing is documented in Experimental Design and declarations. No simultaneous substantially overlapping consideration is permitted. Related manuscripts/preprints and reused material require transparent disclosure. Subscription publishing is offered with no APC; optional Open Choice is not selected.

**Review model: not conclusively verified.** The journal-specific author instructions require author information but do not explicitly name single-blind review. Linked general Springer policies say most journals use single-anonymized review, which is not journal-specific confirmation. The public portal link redirects to authentication; no login, upload or submission object was created. Identified main/SI are prepared with an anonymous fallback. Do not describe single-blind status as confirmed.
