## What explains the cyclicity difference, and what does not

The legacy and current primary numbers come from different saved pipeline versions. The raw/fixed values in the later pipeline already differ from the preprint. It would therefore be false to attribute the whole 87.5→99.2 / 60.0→92.0 transition to normalization. The documented normalization/threshold and version migration is real, but a complete execution-level causal decomposition between the legacy table and current protocol has not been established. Equal query counts are insufficient to infer identical pools or evidence. This limitation is explicitly disclosed in the supplement and main related work. Current within-pipeline ablations provide the valid documented construction contrasts.
