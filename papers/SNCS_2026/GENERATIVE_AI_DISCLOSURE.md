# Generative-AI Disclosure

## Placement decision

Per SN Computer Science / Springer Nature title-page guidance (accessed
2026-08-02): LLMs do not satisfy authorship criteria; use of an LLM should be
documented in the Methods section (or a suitable alternative part). Copy-editing
assistance alone need not be declared; substantive drafting and code assistance
do.

This manuscript places the disclosure in
`\subsection{Reproducibility and Implementation}` (`sec:reproducibility`), the
last subsection of Methodology. It is not duplicated as a Declarations heading.
Portal/cover-letter wording matches the manuscript statement below.

## Disclosure text (as inserted in `main.tex`)

> Anthropic Claude was used for software-development assistance, analysis and
> audit utilities, figure preparation, and drafting and revising portions of the
> manuscript. The author independently reviewed and verified all AI-assisted
> code, analyses, figures, citations, and text against the source materials and
> underlying evidence and assumes full responsibility for the work. No
> generative-AI tool is credited as an author.

## What this disclosure is not

It does not claim AI authorship, autonomous scientific responsibility, or that
routine spellchecking alone constitutes the disclosed assistance.
