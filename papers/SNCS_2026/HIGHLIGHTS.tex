\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=1.5em,itemsep=0.85em]
  \item Preference-graph repair is structurally active but not a validated nDCG gain.
  \item Exact SCIP repair diagnoses greedy edge-deletion MWFAS suboptimality.
  \item No Holm-corrected repaired-vs-unrepaired nDCG gain under this protocol.
  \item Report construction, repair, extraction, and utility as separate evidence.
  \item Missing Holm-corrected evidence is not proof of equivalence.
