## Conflicting or duplicated sources of truth (flagged)

1. **`outputs/pub_vote_cmp_all4/paper_package/` is declared "the canonical evidence package" by four still-live documents** — `README.md` (lines 24-37, 96-98, 183-185, 632), `docs/READ_ME_FIRST_FOR_AI.md` (lines 12, 35), `reports/README.md` ("start here" pointer to `repo_publication_audit.md`), and even `PROJECT_STATUS.md` line 463 (though only in a list of package pointers, not in its main narrative) — **but this pipeline was last touched 2026-03-24 and is referenced nowhere in the actually-submitted `papers/JDIQ_2026/manuscript/main.tex`** (zero string matches for `pub_vote_cmp`). The manuscript's real numeric backbone is `reports/full_calibrated_core/` (2026-07-15), which none of these four documents mention. This is the single highest-severity conflicting-canonical-source finding in this audit.
2. **`papers/JDIQ_2026/MASTER_EVIDENCE_INVENTORY.csv` and `SECTION_EVIDENCE_MAP.csv`** (dated 2026-07-12/14) are cited by `PROJECT_STATUS.md`'s own documentation-authority map as authoritative for "evidence-to-claim mapping," and are described there as "correctly distinguish[ing] canonical (`outputs/pub_vote_cmp_all4/`) from stale" packages. Both predate `full_calibrated_core` by about 3 days and contain zero references to it. `PROJECT_STATUS.md` — otherwise an unusually careful, self-aware document that explicitly tracks known-stale files — has not caught that its own cited evidence map is itself stale on this point.
3. **`reports/exact_ilp_repair_investigation/` vs. `reports/exact_open_source_ilp_repair_investigation/`** — near-identical names, one gitignored/56KB/unused-by-current-code, the other tracked/4.2MB/used by every current test and by `scripts/run_ir_evidence_audit.py`. Easy to cite the wrong one by autocomplete or memory.
4. **`reports/final_revision_task1_pool_cutoff_20260715/` and `.../task4_exact_baseline_fairness_20260715/` are the sole source for two of the six evidence families in `run_ir_evidence_audit.py`, yet are entirely untracked** (blanket-gitignored). There is no conflict in *content* here, but there is a canonical-source *availability* problem: the canonical source exists only on this machine.
5. **The real-LLM studies' "n=120 query-graphs" language** is used identically across `FINAL_IR_EVIDENCE_AUDIT.md`'s proposed manuscript text and could be mistaken for 120 independent observations; the more careful characterization ("6 underlying queries... not a substitute for a larger real-LLM sample") lives only in the sibling `reports/manuscript_reframing_20260729T174326Z/REFRAMING_ANALYSIS.md`, which is not cross-referenced from the audit. Two documents describing the same evidence base at different levels of statistical honesty, with no pointer between them.

## Recommendation

Do not silently pick a winner among conflicting canonical-package claims — that is an authorial decision. This audit's job is to surface the conflict; §1-2 above should be resolved by whoever owns `PROJECT_STATUS.md` and `README.md`, most likely by adding one new row to `PROJECT_STATUS.md`'s documentation-authority map: `full_calibrated_core` supersedes `pub_vote_cmp_all4`/`pub_vote_cmp_v2` as the JDIQ_2026 numeric backbone, with `MASTER_EVIDENCE_INVENTORY.csv`/`SECTION_EVIDENCE_MAP.csv` marked stale pending regeneration.
