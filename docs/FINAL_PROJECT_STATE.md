# Final project state (cold-open context)

This note is for a future reader (human or assistant) opening the repository without prior conversation context. It summarizes what was investigated, what the committed evidence supports, and what it does **not** support.

---

## 1. Final scientific status

### What the method is

The codebase studies **preference graphs** built from multiple rankers (e.g. BM25, TF–IDF, dense retriever scores), optionally aggregated into **pairwise votes** (`scripts/build_votes_file.py`). On each query, a weighted directed graph encodes inferred preferences over a candidate document pool. **Greedy minimum-weight feedback arc set (FAS)** repair removes edges to obtain a DAG; rankings are then extracted (e.g. **Copeland**, **weighted balance**) and optionally combined with score priors via **hybrid RRF-style** methods (`scripts/run_real_experiment.py`, `src/consistency_ranker/greedy_fas.py`, `src/consistency_ranker/baseline_ranking.py`).

### What question we investigated

Whether **graph repair (FAS) on vote-based preference graphs** improves **retrieval ranking quality** (primarily **nDCG@k** on qrels) when rankers disagree, and how that interacts with **how votes are aggregated** (majority-style vs per-ranker edges vs a conflict-mitigating post-filter).

### What the final evidence shows (pinned outputs)

The manuscript-facing, **git-tracked** aggregate is under:

- `outputs/pub_vote_cmp_v2/paper_package/`

Key tables: `tables/table_graph_ndcg_and_consistency.csv`, `tables/table_bootstrap_delta_ndcg.csv`, `tables/table_consistency_qrels_bew.csv`. Interpretation text is mirrored in `outputs/pub_vote_cmp_v2/paper_package/MANUSCRIPT_SUMMARY.md` (also regenerated by `scripts/build_paper_evidence_package.py`).

### Claim **supported** by this evidence

- **Vote aggregation strongly controls cycle prevalence and SCC structure.** Majority-style votes (**ms2**: `min_support=2`, `min_aggregate_margin=0.1`) yield **near-acyclic** graphs; **ms1** (`min_support=1`, `min_aggregate_margin=0.0`) yields **high cyclicity** and **large average largest SCC**. **ms1_drop_mutual** (drop mutual 2-cycle pairs after ms1) restores **low cyclicity** while retaining more edges than ms2 (see Section 3).
- **FAS repair reduces graph–label structural tension** as measured by **backward-edge weight (BEW)** and **pairwise inconsistency count (PIC)** relative to a **qrels-derived reference ranking** on the candidate pool—not a claim about “ground truth,” only alignment with labeled preferences in the pool (`graph_ref_bew_*`, `graph_ref_pic_*` in per-query CSV).
- **Repair is not a free win on nDCG:** under **ms1**, **repaired vs unrepaired Copeland** hybrids show **negative mean ΔnDCG** on SciDocs with a **bootstrap 95% CI strictly below zero**; HotpotQA shows a **small negative mean** with a CI that **touches zero**. **Balance** hybrids show **no meaningful ΔnDCG**. Under **ms2** or **ms1_drop_mutual**, Copeland **ΔnDCG ≈ 0** (repair inactive or rankings coincide).

### Claim **not** supported

- That FAS repair **reliably improves nDCG@k** for these hybrid methods across datasets and vote constructions.
- That **lower BEW/PIC** implies **better user-facing retrieval quality** beyond the specific qrels-aligned definitions used here.
- That results **generalize** beyond the fixed query slices, three rankers, and hyperparameters in `scripts/run_publication_vote_suite.py`.

## 2. Final experimental story (key progression)

1. **Public Hugging Face access** for BEIR-style and other scripted datasets is the intended ingestion path (`scripts/download_datasets.py`, `datasets` / `huggingface-hub` per `pyproject.toml`). An environment audit lives in `docs/DATASET_ACCESS_AUDIT.md` (local placeholders vs Hub fetch).

2. **Initial votes with `min_support=2` (ms2)** produced **near-acyclic** graphs (e.g. SciDocs **~1.7%** cyclic queries in the pinned table), so **FAS removed almost no weight** and **repaired vs unrepaired rankings often coincide** → **repair inactive** for effect sizes.

3. **Switching to `min_support=1` (ms1)** preserved **cross-ranker disagreement**, producing **abundant cycles** (e.g. SciDocs **~97.5%** cyclic, large **avg largest SCC**).

4. In that **high-cycle regime**, **repaired Copeland** was often **neutral or harmful** on **mean nDCG** vs unrepaired (SciDocs ms1: clear negative bootstrap CI; see Section 3).

5. **`ms1_drop_mutual`** (`scripts/postprocess_votes_drop_mutual_pairs.py`) acted as a **middle ground**: removes **mutual 2-cycles** while keeping most ms1 edges → cyclicity drops toward ms2-like levels with **more edges** than ms2.

6. **Structural consistency metrics improved when cycles existed:** for **ms1**, mean **BEW** and especially **PIC** show **pre–post drops** after FAS even when **nDCG** moved the wrong way (tables in `paper_package`).

## 3. Final key quantitative findings

All numbers below are from the **committed** `outputs/pub_vote_cmp_v2/paper_package/tables/table_graph_ndcg_and_consistency.csv` and `table_bootstrap_delta_ndcg.csv` (query counts **after eligibility filtering** in `run_real_experiment.py`).

### SciDocs (n_queries **119** ms2, **120** ms1 / ms1_drop_mutual)

| Variant | % cyclic | Avg largest SCC | Avg n_edges | Mean FAS weight removed | Copeland ΔnDCG (R−U) mean [95% CI] |
|--------|----------|-----------------|------------|-------------------------|-------------------------------------|
| ms2 | 1.68 | 1.042 | 72.4 | ~0.003 | 0 [0, 0] |
| ms1 | 97.5 | 15.56 | 208.6 | ~2.54 | **−0.0091** [**−0.0167**, **−0.0029**] |

**Consistency (ms1 example):** mean BEW pre/post **309.09 → 307.96** (Δ **1.13**); mean PIC **99.84 → 88.08** (Δ **11.76**) — **FAS reduces label-aligned inconsistency on the graph** despite nDCG loss on Copeland.

**Stratified bootstrap (ms1 Copeland):** ΔnDCG is **more negative** for **largest SCC ≥ median** (n=70) than for **< median** (n=50); see `table_bootstrap_delta_ndcg.csv` rows `copeland_scc_high` / `copeland_scc_low`.

### Bootstrap interpretation

`scripts/analyze_publication_vote_deltas.py` resamples **queries with replacement** (default **2000** reps) and reports **percentile CIs** for the **mean per-query ΔnDCG** (repaired − unrepaired). This is **not** a hierarchical or document-level model.

### Consistency metrics (BEW / PIC / FAS weight)

- **BEW / PIC:** Defined in the real experiment pipeline as graph-vs-qrels **backward-edge mass** and **pairwise inconsistency count** (see columns in `*_per_query.csv`). **Pre** = raw preference graph; **post** = DAG after FAS. Larger **Δ (pre − post)** means **more inconsistency removed** by repair.
- **FAS weight removed:** Sum of weights of deleted edges; **~0** under near-acyclic **ms2** / **ms1_drop_mutual** on HotpotQA indicates **little or no repair**.

## 4. Exact final paper framing (manuscript-facing)

Use wording along these lines (consistent with `MANUSCRIPT_SUMMARY.md`):

1. **Vote aggregation controls cycle prevalence** in multi-ranker preference graphs; majority thresholds produce near-DAGs, while per-ranker edges produce dense cycles unless conflicts are explicitly abstained (e.g. mutual-pair dropping).

2. **FAS repair reduces graph–label inconsistency** (BEW, PIC vs qrels-aligned reference) **when cycles exist**.

3. **That structural improvement does not reliably translate into better nDCG@k** for the reported hybrid Copeland/balance methods; in **high-conflict (ms1)** regimes, **Copeland repair can hurt** SciDocs with high confidence, and **Balance** shows negligible deltas.

4. **Harm is concentrated in larger-SCC queries** under SciDocs ms1 (exploratory stratification; interpret with care).

5. **Frame repair** as altering **structural consistency** and **conditionally** affecting **ranking metrics**—**not** as a uniformly beneficial reranker.

## 7. Remaining caveats and open issues

- **Sample size:** SciDocs **~120** queries, HotpotQA **52** after eligibility; stratified subsets (e.g. SCC low) can be **small or empty** (see `n_queries` in bootstrap table for `copeland_scc_low` when **0**).
- **ms1_drop_mutual** is a **documented post hoc** construction—not tuned on a validation split.
- **Three rankers, fixed vote hyperparameters** (`run_publication_vote_suite.py`); generalization unknown.
- **BEW/PIC** are **one** consistency lens tied to **qrels over the candidate pool**; they do not prove relevance correctness.
- **Test suite:** `pytest` reports **186 passed** in the reference environment after aligning `tests/test_baseline_ranking.py` with current API names (`weighted_out_minus_in_ranking`, `hybrid_rrf_fas_regularized_ranking`). A full-tree `ruff check .` may still surface **pre-existing** lint issues in other files unless the project cleans them globally.
- **Local data:** Fresh clones may contain only placeholders under `data/raw/` and `data/processed/` until `download_datasets.py` / `prepare_datasets.py` are run (`docs/DATASET_ACCESS_AUDIT.md`).
